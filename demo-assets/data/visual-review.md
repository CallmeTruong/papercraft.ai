## Curation decisions

- Keep the recorded baseline images beside the curated results so the tradeoffs remain inspectable.
- Use manually selected detail regions and increased color budgets for the colored modes. This is a curated demo, not proof of automatic eye detection.
- Use the light-paper Stencil variant for the puppy; the original dark-paper attempt was rejected by fabrication checks.
- Use Warm light with 55% assumed paper transmission for the main white-paper comparison. Colored light remains a separate preview choice. Exact controls are recorded separately.
- Preserve warnings, failed attempts and AI fallbacks in the accompanying JSON rather than relabelling them as universal success.
- MediaPipe reported landmarks for the puppy, but the configured model targets human faces. This is an unverified proposal, not proof of reliable pet landmark detection.

All final geometry reports passed at their recorded physical sizes and default material profile. Physical cutting, assembly, durability and optical calibration remain untested.
